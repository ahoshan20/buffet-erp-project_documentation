Ingredients, Stock & Waste

Ingredients, stock, low stock, stock adjustment and waste & preserve.

Who uses this page:Storekeeper

Ingredients and items

Setup

Items > Add Ingredient/Item

Choose the Type: Ingredient (used in recipes) or Item (something you sell or use as it is). Then fill in:

FieldMeaning
Name, Code, CategoryHow the ingredient is found. Categories are added in Items > Add Ingredient Category.
Purchase UnitThe unit you buy in (for example Kg).
Consumption Unit and Conversion RateThe unit the kitchen uses (for example G), and how many of them make one purchase unit (1000). For an Item this is the Sale Unit.
Cost Per Unit / Purchase PriceWhat it costs.
Stock Alert QtyBelow this the ingredient shows in Low Stock.
Minimum Purchase QtyPurchase forecasting never suggests buying less than this.
Opening StockHow much you have when you start.

Units are added in Items > Add Ingredient Unit and listed in Items > List Ingredient Unit; categories are in Items > List Ingredient Category. Many ingredients can be added at once with Bulk Upload on List Ingredient/Item.

Stock

Check

Stock > Ingredient Stock and Low Stock

Ingredient Stock shows the stock of every ingredient. Low Stock lists only those at or below their Stock Alert Qty, with tiles for the number of low items, their value and the items that are out of stock.

Correct

Stock > Add Stock Adjustment

Use this when the counted stock differs from the system (for example after a stock count).

  1. Check the Adjustment Date and choose the Responsible Person.
  2. Search and add each ingredient. Enter the Quantity and choose Plus (add to stock) or Minus (deduct from stock). Note on Minus adjustments: You cannot deduct more quantity than the current available stock. If a Minus quantity exceeds current inventory, the system blocks the adjustment with the error: "Adjustment quantity exceeds available stock for [Ingredient Name]."
  3. Write the Reason and click Submit.

Saved adjustments are in Stock > List Stock Adjustment. Spoiled or thrown-away stock is recorded under Waste & Preserve instead (below), so it shows in the Waste Report.

Waste & Preserve

Record

Waste & Preserve > Add Waste & Preserve

Choose the Responsible Person and the Item Type. There is no reason list; write any explanation in the Note.

  • Production Food Menu: leftovers of a production. Meal Time and Production Date are required. Enter Waste Qty and Preserve Qty; together they cannot be more than what is left of the production. Waste is only recorded; Preserve Qty is added back to Food Menu Stock.
  • Preserve Food Menu: waste of food that was preserved earlier and is in Food Menu Stock. Pick the food menus yourself; only Waste Qty is entered, and it is taken out of Food Menu Stock. A menu with no stock is refused ("… has no stock in Food Menu Stock.").
  • Ingredient: spoiled or expired raw ingredients. Enter Waste Qty and Loss Amount. Waste Qty cannot be more than the current stock.
  • Item: wasted items. Same fields and rule as Ingredient.

Click Submit, or Save Draft to finish later. Only submitted records change stock. List Waste & Preserve shows Active (submitted) or Draft records.

Common messages

MessageWhy it happensWhat to do
"Adjustment quantity exceeds available stock for [Ingredient]."You selected Minus consumption status for a quantity greater than current on-hand stock.Check physical counts and adjust the Minus quantity so it does not exceed current stock.
"… has no stock in Food Menu Stock."You tried to log waste on a preserved food menu that has 0 preserved quantity.Check the Food Menu Stock list under Production before logging waste against preserved food.
