Login & Open Register

Sign in with email or PIN and open or join a register before using the POS.

Who uses this page:All users
Before you start

Your manager gives you an email and password, or a 6-digit login PIN. To use the POS, a counter must already exist (Settings > Add Counter).

Step 1

Log in

The login page has two tabs:

  • Login using email: enter your email and password, tick Remember me on your own device only, and click Login.
  • Login using PIN: type your 6-digit PIN, one number per box. You are logged in as soon as you type the sixth number.

Your restaurant may show only one of the two tabs (set in Settings > Settings, Login Option).

MessageWhyWhat to do
"The provided credentials are incorrect"The email or password is wrong.Check the spelling, or use Forgot Password?.
"Invalid PIN"No user has that PIN.Re-type it. Your manager can see or change your PIN on your employee record.
Step 2

Open or join a register

Go to Sale > POS. If you have no open register, you are taken to the Open Register page. It has two tabs:

  • New Register: choose your Counter, then type the money you start with in the box for each payment account (for example cash in the drawer under the cash account, 0 for the others). The Total Opening Balance is added up for you. Click Submit.
  • Existing Register: a register is already open on that counter (another cashier opened it). Choose the Counter and click Submit to join it.
FieldRequired?What to enter
CounterYesThe counter you work at. The + button opens Add Counter.
One box per payment accountNoThe money in that account at the start. Leave 0 if none. Negative numbers are not allowed.

If you see "No active payment account found.", ask your manager to add one in Payment Account > Add Payment Account.

Step 3

Start working

After the register opens, Sale > POS shows your counter name, the current meal time and the floor plan. Continue with POS: Check-in & Billing. At the end of your shift, see How to Close the Register.
