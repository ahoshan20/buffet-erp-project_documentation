Reservations, Cancellations & Refunds

Add a reservation, take an advance, confirm online bookings, cancel and refund.

Who uses this page:CashierManager
Before you start

Meal times with prices and an Advance Payment Amount must exist (How to Set Meal Times & Prices), and a floor plan must be set up. Without a floor plan, Add Reservation shows "No floor plan configured. Please set up a floor plan in Settings first."

Add a reservation

Step 1

Reservation > Add Reservation

  1. Check the Reservation Date and choose the Meal Time. If a meal time's scheduled dining hours have already passed for today, it is marked with (Ended for today) and cannot be chosen. View Food Menu shows what that meal time serves.
  2. Choose the Customer, or click + to add a new one (Customer Name and Phone Number are required).
  3. Set Adult Guests, Child Guests and Senior Guests. At least 1 guest is needed.
  4. Under Table Selection, choose the Area/Floor and click tables to pick seats until every guest has a seat. Green seats are available, yellow are selected, red are already reserved.
  5. The Booking Summary shows the subtotal, Discounts (after a customer and meal time are chosen), tax, Total Amount and Minimum Amount To Pay.
  6. Take the advance: choose the Counter, the Payment Account and the Paid Amount (Add Payment for a second method). For a walk-in with no advance, tick Walk-in Guest (Advance pay optional).
  7. Click Submit, or Print & Save to print the receipt as well.

How the minimum advance is worked out: each meal time (and weekend or special day price) has an Advance Payment Amount: either a fixed amount per guest (for example 20) or a percentage of the booking (for example 10%). The total is rounded to a whole amount and is never more than the bill.

Find and change reservations

List

Reservation > Reservation List

Search by name, number or reference, or filter by Meal Time and Reservation Status. From a row you can view, edit, delete or Print Receipt. Settled and cancelled reservations cannot be edited.

To cancel a reservation, edit it and set Reservation Status to Cancelled. The status can be Pending, Confirmed or Cancelled.

Edit a reservation

Edit

What can and cannot change

To adjust a booking before guests arrive, go to Reservation > Reservation List and click the Edit (pencil) icon:

  • What CAN be changed:
    • Reservation Date & Meal Time: You can change the date or move to another active meal time. Note: A booking that is already booked for an ended meal time today can keep its own meal time, but you cannot move a booking into a different ended meal time today.
    • Customer details: Switch or edit the customer assigned to the booking.
    • Guest headcounts: Increase or decrease Adult, Child, or Senior counts.
    • Table assignments: Reassign or add seats on the interactive floor plan.
    • Notes: update the guest's special requests.
    • Promotions & Coupons: Apply or remove eligible discounts.
    • Advance payments: Take an additional deposit if guest counts increase.
  • What CANNOT be changed:
    • Cancelled bookings: Cancelled reservations are permanently locked ("Cancelled reservations cannot be edited.").
    • Settled bookings: Once a booking is checked in and settled at the POS, it cannot be modified here ("Settled reservations cannot be edited.").
    • Deleting a paid booking: a reservation that has received payments cannot be deleted ("This reservation has received payments in the books, so it cannot be deleted. Cancel it instead: the fee is kept and the rest of the advance refunded."). Cancel it instead; the refund then appears under Sale > Refund.

Online reservations

Online

Reservation > Online Reservations

Guests can book on your public booking page (its link is under Website Settings > Online Reservation Setting, Online Reservation Link). The page asks a small sum (for example 7 + 5) to block spam bookings, and can take payment online.

  • On the public online booking page, any meal time whose dining hours have already ended for today shows Ended for today and cannot be booked.
  • The tiles show Total Bookings, Pending, Confirmed and Cancelled.
  • For a Pending booking click Confirm (or Cancel). Only a pending booking can be confirmed or cancelled here.
  • A booking that is not paid within the Payment Time Limit (minutes) becomes Expired. If money arrives after that, the filter Paid after expiry (refund) shows it so you can refund it.

Cancellations, no-shows and refunds

Refund

Sale > Refund

When a booking with an advance is cancelled, or marked No Show at the POS, part of the advance can be kept as a fee. The fee is the No-Show & Cancellation Fee in Settings > Settings (a flat amount, or a % of the booking). The rest is owed back to the guest and appears in Sale > Refund (and as "Refunds pending" on the dashboard).

  1. Open Sale > Refund. Filter by date or Status, or search by reservation, customer or phone.
  2. Open the refund. Click Pay Refund, choose the Payment Account the money leaves from, and submit. The full amount is paid at once.
  3. Or click Deny Refund: the money stays in the account and is recorded as income. This cannot be undone.
