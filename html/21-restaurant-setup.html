Restaurant Setup Checklist

Counters, floors, tables, printers, denominations, weekend and special days.

Who uses this page:OwnerAdmin
Do it in this order

Each step uses what the step before created. All pages are under Settings unless another menu is named.

#PageWhat to enter
1Settings and White LabelRestaurant name, currency, time zone, logos. See Settings, Tax & White Label.
2Tax SettingYour tax rates, if you collect tax.
3Payment Account > Add Payment AccountAt least a cash account. See Payment Accounts & Accounting.
4Add PrinterReceipt and kitchen printers. See Equipment & Printers.
5Add Area/FloorArea/Floor Name (for example "1st Floor"), description, status.
6Add TableTable Name, the Area/Floor, Seat Capacity, status.
7Floor/Area Plan DesignPlace the tables as they stand in the room and click Save Setting.
8Add CounterCounter Name, the Area/Floor it serves (its floor plan is shown on that counter's POS), the Invoice Printer and Bill Printer, status.
9Add DenominationOptional. A list of the notes and coins of your currency (Amount, description, status). No other screen uses this list at the moment; closing the register does not ask you to count notes.
10Add Meal Time, Weekend Setting, Add Special DayMeal times and their prices. See How to Set Meal Times & Prices.
11Items menuUnits, ingredient categories, ingredients, food menu categories and food menus. See Ingredients and Food Menus.
12Kitchen > Add KitchenEach kitchen, its printer and its food menu categories. See Production & Kitchen Panel.
13Roles & Permissions, EmployeeRoles, designations and user logins. See Staff, Roles & Salary.
14Promotion & DiscountOptional discounts and manager PINs. See Customers, Promotions & Loyalty.
15Website Settings > Online Reservation SettingOptional public booking page (below).
16SMS Setting, Email Settings, WhatsApp SettingsOptional, for messages to customers.

Saved records are in Settings > List Area/Floor, List Table, List Counter, List Denomination, List Printer and List Meal Time.

Online

Website Settings > Online Reservation Setting

  • General Settings: the page heading, description, button text (for example "Reserve Now") and background image (1920×600 px recommended).
  • Online Payment: which payment accounts guests can pay with, and the Payment Time Limit (minutes). An online booking that is not paid in this time expires.
  • Contact Information and Footer Setting: shown on the public page.
  • SEO: the title, description and keywords search engines show.
  • Who will be notified after an online reservation: the users who get a notification.
  • Online Reservation Link: the address of your public booking page. Click Copy to share it.

SMS, Email & WhatsApp Settings

SMS

Settings > SMS Setting

  1. Choose the SMS Service Provider: Twilio, Nexmo (Vonage), Provider (Generic Gateway), MSG91, Infobip, Gupshup, SMSGatewayHub, Kaleyra, MiMSMS, Bulk SMS BD or Mobishastra.
  2. Fill in the fields that appear for that provider, from your account with them (for Twilio: SID, Token and Twilio Number).
  3. Set Status to Active and click Submit.
  4. To test, open Test SMS, choose the Country Code, enter the Number and Message, and click Send Test SMS.

Until a provider is set, SMS screens show "Please enable and configure SMS service provider first."

Email

Settings > Email Settings

  1. On Configure Mail, choose the Mail Type: SMTP, Mailgun, SendGrid, Amazon SES, Postmark, Elastic Email or Sendinblue (Brevo).
  2. Fill in the fields for that type. For SMTP: Host Address, Mail Port, Encryption, Mail Username, Mail Password, Mail From and Mail From Name. The other types ask for their key (for example API Key or Server Token) and the sender.
  3. Set Status and click Submit.
  4. To test, open Test Mail, enter the Email Address, Subject and Message, and click Send Test Email.
WhatsApp

Settings > WhatsApp Settings

  1. Choose the WhatsApp Service Provider: Twilio, RCSoft, or Disable.
  2. For Twilio enter the Account SID, Auth Token and Twilio Number. For RCSoft enter the App Key and Auth Key.
  3. Click Submit.
  4. To test, open Test WhatsApp Message, enter the Number (with country code) and Message, and click Send WhatsApp Message.
