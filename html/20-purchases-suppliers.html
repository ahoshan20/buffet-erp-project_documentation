Purchases & Suppliers

Suppliers, purchases, returns, supplier payments, supplier advance and expenses.

Who uses this page:StorekeeperAccountant

Suppliers

Setup

Supplier > Add Supplier

Enter the Supplier Name, Contact Person, Phone Number, email and address. Opening Balance is what you already owe this supplier when you start. Set the Status and click Submit. Many suppliers can be added with Bulk Upload.

Buying stock

Optional

Purchase > Purchase Forecast

Works out which ingredients to buy for coming meal times, from past sales (or from the food menus' production amounts when there is no sales history) and the Current Stock. It never suggests less than an ingredient's Minimum Purchase Qty. Saved forecasts are in List Purchase Forecast.

Step 1

Purchase > Add Purchase

  1. Choose the Supplier (+ adds a new one) and check the Date. The Reference No is filled in.
  2. Under Item, search and add each ingredient or item. Enter the Unit Price and Quantity; the Total is worked out.
  3. Under Payment Details: add a Discount if any (amount or %), check the G. Total, choose the Account you pay from and the Paid amount. Whatever is not paid shows as Due and is owed to the supplier. If the supplier holds an advance from you, it can be used here as a payment.
  4. Click Submit, or Save Draft to finish later.
Step 2

Pay what you owe: Supplier Payment > Add Supplier Payment

Choose the Supplier. The form shows the Due Amount and any Advance Amount. Enter the Pay Amount, choose the Payment Method, add a Note and click Submit.

Advance

Supplier Advance > Add Supplier Advance

Money paid to a supplier before you buy. Choose the Supplier (the form shows the Due Amount and Advance Balance), the Type: Payment (you pay the supplier) or Refund (the supplier gives advance money back), the account and the Amount. The advance can later pay part of a purchase.

Returning stock to a supplier

Return

Purchase Return > Add Purchase Return

  1. Choose the Supplier, then the Purchase the goods came from (only active purchases are listed). Check the Return Date.
  2. Choose the Status:
    • Taken by supplier Money Returned: the supplier gives credit at the Return Price. It first lowers what you still owe on that purchase; the rest is either refunded into a payment account (Refund to a payment account, choose the Refund Account) or Keep as Supplier Advance.
    • Taken by supplier Product Returned: the supplier replaces the same goods.
    • Taken by supplier Product Not Returned: the supplier took the goods but has not settled yet. Settle it later from List Purchase Return with Settle (as money or as replacement).
  3. For each item enter the Return Quantity (and Return Price). Write the Reason for the return in the note and click Submit.

Expenses

Expense

Expense > Add Expense

For money spent that is not stock (gas, cleaning, repairs). Add categories first in Add Expense Category. Then choose the Date, Expense Category, Amount, the Employee responsible, the Payment Method and an optional Note (up to 500 characters). Click Submit.

Where saved records are

  • Supplier > List Supplier, Supplier Payment > List Supplier Payment, Supplier Advance > List Supplier Advance.
  • Expense > List Expense and Expense > List Expense Category.
  • Purchase > List Purchase and Purchase Return > List Purchase Return.
