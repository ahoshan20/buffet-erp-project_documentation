Customers, Promotions & Loyalty

Add customers, set up the six promotion types and loyalty points.

Who uses this page:ManagerCashier

Customers

Add

Customer > Add Customer

FieldRequired?Why it matters
Customer NameYesShown on bookings and bills.
Phone Number (with country code)Yes4 to 15 digits. Used to find the customer at the POS.
Email, Address, State, NotesNoState is needed for the tax breakdown when tax is worked out by state.
Date of Birth, Anniversary DateNoNeeded for the Birthday / Anniversary discount. Cannot be a future date.
StatusYesActive or Inactive.

Customers can also be added directly during booking in Add Reservation or at the POS via Instant Reservation.

List

Customer > Customer List

Manage your customer database, check contact details, and follow up with diners:

  • Customer Type: filter the list. The choices are Regular and the title of each Membership Discount you created under Promotion & Discount.
  • Show Inactive: click this button to see inactive customers (it then reads Show Active).
  • Next to each phone number, the phone icon calls the customer and the WhatsApp icon opens a WhatsApp chat with them.
  • The eye icon opens Customer Details: name, phone, email, date of birth, anniversary date, address, notes, and who added the customer and when.
  • Bulk Upload: Click Bulk Upload to import large customer lists from an Excel file at once.

Promotions and discounts

Add

Promotion & Discount > Add Promotion & Discount

Choose the Type, enter a Title, the Applicable Meal Time (All Meal Time by default), the Discount (an amount such as 500, or a percentage such as 10%) and the Status. The other fields depend on the type:

TypeWhen it appliesExtra fields
DiscountEveryone, between two dates.Start Date, End Date (required).
Membership DiscountRegular customers who have visited enough times and spend enough.Minimum Visit Count, Minimum Bill Amount, Usage Limit (how many times one customer can use it).
Birthday / Anniversary DiscountOn the customer's birthday or anniversary (same day and month).None. The customer needs a Date of Birth or Anniversary Date.
Group DiscountWhen one reservation has at least this many guests (adults, seniors and children together).Minimum Guest Count.
CouponWhen the guest gives the code.Coupon Code (letters, numbers, - and _; saved in capitals), dates, Minimum Bill Amount, Usage Limit (total uses; empty = unlimited).
Manual DiscountWhen a manager approves it at the POS.The discount here is the Maximum Discount. Add each approver with Add User and give them a 4-digit PIN (Generate PIN makes one). Only employees who can log in are listed.

At the POS and in reservations, available discounts appear under Discounts & loyalty; ones that do not apply show why (for example "Needs 5 visits" or "No birthday / anniversary today"). Coupons and manual discounts have their own boxes. See POS: Check-in & Billing.

Rules

Strict promotion validation rules

When creating or modifying promotions, Buffet ERP enforces these business rules:

  • End Date not in the past: When setting or updating an End Date, it cannot be in the past ("The End Date cannot be in the past."). On edit, an existing expired promotion can keep its past end date if unchanged, but moving it requires today or a future date.
  • Flat discount cannot exceed Minimum Bill: For Flat discounts under Coupon or Membership promotions, the discount value cannot be larger than the Minimum Bill Amount ("A flat Discount cannot be more than the Minimum Bill Amount.").
  • Usage Limit cannot be less than existing uses: When editing a coupon, the Usage Limit cannot be reduced below the number of times it has already been used ("This coupon has already been used N times, so the Usage Limit cannot be less than N."). The form displays Used so far: N times for tracking.
  • Single use per booking: A promotion is counted only once per booking, whether it was applied in Add Reservation or at the POS checkout.
  • Cancelled/Expired bookings release uses: If a customer cancels a booking or an online reservation expires before payment, the promotion use is automatically returned back to the available pool.
  • Membership limit is per customer: For a Membership promotion, the Usage Limit specifies how many times each individual customer can benefit from the discount.
Manage

Promotion & Discount > List Promotion & Discount

Search, review, edit, or remove configured promotions and discount cards:

  • Filter by Type: Filter by All Types, Discount, Membership Discount, Birthday / Anniversary Discount, Manual Discount, Group Discount, or Coupon.
  • Show Inactive: click this button to see inactive promotions (it then reads Show Active).
  • Search: Search promotions instantly by Title or Coupon Code.
  • Actions:
    • View Details: Open the modal to review discount percentage, applicability, meal times, visit limits, and authorized manager PINs.
    • Edit: Modify promotional dates, discount percentages, or add authorized PIN users.
    • Delete: Permanently delete a promotion after confirming the warning dialog ("Are you sure you want to delete this record? This action cannot be undone.").

Loyalty points

Setup

Turn loyalty points on

  1. In Settings > Settings set Loyalty Point to Enable, the Loyalty Point Rate (1 point equals how much money) and the Minimum Loyalty Point to Redeem.
  2. In each meal time set the Loyalty Point a customer earns when their reservation for that meal time is settled at the POS.

Earning happens only when a bill is settled at the POS. Redeeming: in the invoice window, the Loyalty Points box shows the customer's balance; enter the points to redeem. At least the minimum must be used at once, no more than the balance, and no more than the amount due. The receipt shows the points earned and the new balance.

Reports: Available Loyalty Point Report and Usage Loyalty Point Report.
