Payment Accounts & Accounting

Payment accounts, deposits, transfers and what the accounting pages show.

Who uses this page:AccountantOwner

Payment accounts

Setup

Payment Account > Add Payment Account

A payment account is a place money is kept or received: the cash drawer, a bank account, a card machine, mobile banking or an online payment service. Every sale, purchase, expense and salary is paid from or into one of them.

  1. Choose the Account Type: Cash, Bank, Credit Card, Mobile Banking, or an online service (PayPal, Stripe, Razorpay, Paystack, Paytm, Flutterwave, SSLCommerz, SenangPay, PhonePe, MyFatoorah).
  2. Enter the Title users will see, and the Opening Balance. For a bank add the Bank Name, Branch Name and Account Number (the account number must contain digits only; letters or dashes will trigger "The Account Number must contain digits only.").
  3. For an online service, enter the keys from that service's account and the Mode. For Mobile Banking you can choose a Provider (bKash or Nagad) to take online payments.
  4. Click Submit.

All accounts are in Payment Account > List Payment Account. Sorting Payment Accounts sets the order the active accounts are listed in.

Online payment keys & webhook addresses

  • Identified currency required: Online payment gateways only work if your currency is recognized in Settings > Settings (see Settings & White Label).
  • Webhook URL appears after saving keys: For gateways that authenticate notifications (such as Stripe, Razorpay, and PayPal), the copyable Webhook URL field only appears after the webhook secret key or webhook ID is entered and saved. Once saved, copy the displayed Webhook URL and paste it into the payment gateway developer console so the provider can confirm successful customer payments.

Money

Deposits, withdrawals and transfers

  • Add Deposit Or Withdraw: the owner puts money into an account or takes it out. Choose Deposit Or Withdraw, the Payment Account, the Amount and a Note.
  • Add Account Transfer: move money between two accounts (for example drawer to bank). Choose From and To, enter the Amount; the form shows each account's balance after the transfer. Click Submit Transfer.
  • Saved records are in List Deposit Or Withdraw and List Account Transfer.

Accounting

You do not type accounting entries for daily work

Every sale, advance, refund, purchase, expense, salary and stock change writes its accounting entry by itself. For example, when you settle a cash bill, the cash account goes up and sales income goes up by the same amount. Accountants use the pages below to check these entries.

Overview

Accounting > Dashboard

Shows Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit, Supplier Payable and Total Receivables, a Revenue & Expenses chart, the Expense Breakdown, payment account balances, Top Expense Categories and Balance Alerts.

Accounts

Accounting > Chart of Accounts

The list of all accounts in five groups: Assets (what you own), Liabilities (what you owe), Equity (the owner's share), Income and Expenses. Each row shows the opening and closing balance.

Entries

Accounting > Journal Entries

Every entry with its number, date, description, where it came from (POS Sales, Reservation, Purchase …), the Debit and Credit amounts (always equal) and its status. Filter by date. Add Journal Entry is only for corrections an accountant makes by hand.

Control

Accounting > Accounting Settings

  • Lock Date: nothing dated on or before this day can be posted, edited or reversed. Clear it to reopen.
  • Fiscal Year Start Month and Go-live Date.

Every change here is recorded in Accounting Audit Log with the old and new value. The accounting reports (Account Statement, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Transaction History, Profit And Loss, Payment Reconciliation, Customer Ledger, Supplier Ledger) are under Accounting > Reports.
